# Privacy Policy – Coda Task Logger (Navy & Gold)

Coda Task Logger does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.

All data generated by the extension is stored locally in the user’s browser using standard browser storage. The extension does not use external servers, analytics services, tracking pixels, or third-party scripts.

The extension only runs on coda.io pages and is used solely to observe user-initiated interactions for task logging purposes. No data leaves the user’s device.

If the extension is removed, all locally stored data can be deleted by clearing the browser’s site data for coda.io.

## Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you may contact the developer via the Chrome Web Store listing.
